January 28, 1989. Elsa Brandvold, 54, owner of Greyhorn Lodge, walks out into a snowstorm on the night of the Winter Carnival gala. Next morning she is found at the bottom of Hollow Gully, 9 meters below the footbridge to her chalet. The sheriff rules it an accident. Thirty-seven years later, the mountain gives back an undeveloped roll of film, her green notebook and a strongbox nobody can open.
