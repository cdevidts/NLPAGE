# Cold case file: who killed the theater owner on closing night?

Cold case file murder mystery game: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4604988351

A theater owner is found dead on the closing night of his own play. Police arrest the playwright, let him go two days later, and the case goes cold. Thirteen years on, builders find the owner's missing notebook and two half-burned pages behind a loose panel.

The case is fiction: it's Cold Case File No. 01, a printable game. You get the whole file (39 evidence documents) and work out who did it, how and why. 1-6 players, about 2-3 hours, no host. English and Spanish versions included. SheetSmithChile on Etsy.
