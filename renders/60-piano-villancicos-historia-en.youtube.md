December 16, 1965. Gemini 6A astronauts Wally Schirra and Tom Stafford report a strange object in orbit, with a pilot in a red suit. Then Schirra plays a small harmonica, Stafford shakes a set of bells, and Jingle Bells becomes the first song played in space (Guinness World Records). The harmonica and bells are now at the Smithsonian. The song itself was published in 1857 by James Lord Pierpont as "The One Horse Open Sleigh", and it wasn't written as a Christmas song.
