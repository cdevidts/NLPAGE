Vienna, May 7, 1824. Ludwig van Beethoven, almost completely deaf, stands by the stage at the premiere of his Ninth Symphony, beating time for an orchestra he can't hear. The players had been told to follow the other conductor. At the end (accounts differ on the exact moment) he is still conducting, and the contralto Caroline Unger turns him around to see the audience applauding. The symphony's finale, Ode to Joy, was adopted as the Anthem of Europe in 1972.
